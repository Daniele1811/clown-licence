Before you start

You must apply for a licence if:

  • if you work with children
  • you trade in a public place

The cost of a clown licence is £120.00 per year.

To apply for a clown licence, you will need to:

  • provide your personal details together with a valid passport or driving licence
  • evidence your skills related to operating as a clown
  • provide a list of places you have worked for the last 5 years
  • provide the names and contact details of 2 referees, whom we will contact before your licence can be issued to you

You can also register by post.

The online service is also available in Welsh (ar gael yn Gymraeg).

You can’t register for this service if you’re in the UK illegally.
