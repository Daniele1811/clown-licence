What identity document will you provide?


You’ll need to prove your identity with a valid UK passport or driving licence
You’ll need to provide a valid UK passport, or if using a passport or identity card from a country other than the UK, provide evidence of your right to remain in the UK
If you don’t have a UK driving licence, you must provide your passport or national identity card as proof of identity
